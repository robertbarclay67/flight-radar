Version history

Flight Radar for Android: what's new

Every public release of the Android app, newest first. Install a new version over the old one; settings and recent flights are kept.

Version 1.0.7

  • POINT: hold your phone up to the sky and see every plane labelled. Tap the bullseye at the top of the map: the camera view puts a tag on each aircraft with its flight, altitude and distance, using your GPS, the phone's compass and gyro and the camera's field of view, with positions moved on to the present second. The plane nearest the crosshair lights up with a card showing its type and speed; tap it for the full flight view. Pinch or 1× / 2× / 5× to zoom, 10 / 25 / 50 mile range, portrait or landscape. Our biggest addition yet.
  • Flights open right on the map you're looking at: tap a plane on the map (or in the list) and its flight opens at the same place and zoom. Back returns to the main map exactly as you left it.
  • Gate numbers and taxiway letters in the flight view, so you can follow a plane on the ground to its gate. Planes on the ground open zoomed in close.
  • A new top bar: the aircraft count, FIT (zoom to the approach area of the nearest airport) and APPR (follow the selected plane, zoomed to its altitude), then Airports, WX and the menu, evenly spaced. Tap FIT or APPR again to go back to the view you had.
  • - / + buttons on the map with the range shown between them, in even steps (1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 250 miles). Pinch and the mouse wheel still zoom.
  • DETAILS and INFO buttons at the top of the flight view and airport pages tuck the panel away (on phones, clear of the navigation buttons).
  • Fixed a plane re-selecting itself after you tapped away from it or picked another one.
  • The aircraft count fits three digits.

Version 1.0.6

  • Gate numbers, taxiway letters and terminal names when you zoom into an airport, on the main map and airport pages (from OpenStreetMap).
  • Folder tabs for more map: VIEW tucks the map buttons away; on the flight view and airport pages, DETAILS and INFO tuck the panel away for a full-screen map. Each remembers how you left it. The altitude legend stays on screen.
  • Landscape: the flight view and airport pages show the map and details side by side, and the main map's buttons fit with the phone turned sideways.
  • Buttons and tabs stay clear of the phone's navigation buttons and gesture bar.
  • The aircraft count and Airports button sit together, centred at the top.
  • FLT and FIT re-fit the map when the window or screen changes shape.

Version 1.0.5

  • The flight view shows the time since takeoff and the estimated time left, between the distance flown and the distance to go.
  • Airport events (Settings): ground stops, delay programs, delays and closures at US airports, live from the FAA.
  • G-AIRMETs (Settings): IFR, turbulence and icing forecasts from aviationweather.gov on a map, now and out to 12 hours.
  • Map buttons on airport pages no longer stretch across the panel.

Version 1.0.4

  • The altitude legend moved so it no longer covers the map buttons on shorter screens.

Version 1.0.3

  • Photos of the actual aircraft from Planespotters.net, credited to the photographer. When there isn't one, a stock photo in the manufacturer's colours, labelled as such.
  • Airline logos in the flight view and the aircraft list; airport logos on airport pages and in the airport picker.
  • Real photos of thousands of airports (PHOTO on the INFO tab).
  • ALL / AIRLINE / PRIVATE traffic filter for busy areas.
  • RADAR / STREET / SAT, FLT and APPR buttons light up while in use. FLT shows the whole route and is the view a flight opens with; APPR zooms to suit the aircraft's altitude.
  • "Landed at ... and at the gate" instead of "Signal lost" when a flight has landed.
  • The path flown no longer includes the aircraft's previous flight; Pacific routes are drawn across the date line.

Version 1.0.2 and earlier

  • The first versions: live map and radar, flight view with route and live data, owner lookup, rain radar, 9,000+ airports with frequencies, services and weather, and recent flights.
