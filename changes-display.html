Version history

Flight Radar display: what's new

Every firmware release for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-7, newest first. Updating from the website keeps your settings.

Firmware 4.0.9

  • Flights open right on the map you're looking at. Tap a plane and its flight opens at the same place and zoom, showing just that aircraft with its path and route, and the details in the side panel. X on the map goes back to all aircraft, exactly where you were.
  • INFO tab tucks the flight details away for a full-width map while the plane stays selected, so you can follow it for as long as you like.
  • Gate numbers and taxiway letters in the flight view, so you can follow a plane on the ground all the way to its gate. Planes on the ground open zoomed in close.
  • APPR on the main map now switches to whichever plane you select, and turns off when you clear the selection.

Firmware 4.0.8

  • A top bar on the main map, like the apps: the aircraft count, FIT, APPR and WX, evenly spaced. With the aircraft list tucked away it adds AIRPORTS, the - / + range and the menu.
  • FIT zooms the main map to the approach area of the airport you're at (or the nearest one). APPR follows the plane you've selected, the range set by its altitude. - / + adjust either; tap it again to go back to the view you had.
  • RADAR / STREET / SAT moved into the left stack with ALL / AIRLINE / PRIVATE, all behind the VIEW tab.
  • Flight view: APPR now stays on and re-zooms as the plane climbs or descends; tap it again to turn it off. - / + take over from FLT and APPR; FLT goes back to the whole route.
  • Recent flights show each airline's logo.

Firmware 4.0.7

  • Gate numbers and taxiway letters on airport maps, from OpenStreetMap: taxiway letters as you zoom in, gate numbers one step further. Built in for the 64 major airports (over 10,000 gates); other airports download them on the first visit.
  • Folder tabs on the main screen. VIEW (left) tucks the ALL / AIRLINE / PRIVATE buttons away; the altitude key stays. LIST (right) tucks the aircraft list away and the map or radar fills the whole screen, with a small - / + range control top right. Both remember how you left them.
  • The aircraft count is now in the panel's header, next to FLIGHT RADAR.
  • The widest range is now 250 miles (500 km). The 500-mile view used most of the board's memory; dropping it, and sharing map memory between screens, makes room for the full-width map with memory to spare.

Firmware 4.0.6

  • The flight view shows the time since takeoff and the estimated time left, between the distance flown and the distance to go (for example "6m | ~3h44"; ~ marks an estimate).
  • Airport events (menu): ground stops, ground delay programs, departure and arrival delays, closures, de-icing and notices at US airports, live from the FAA's NAS status, most serious first. Tap an airport for its page.
  • G-AIRMETs (menu): the FAA's graphical AIRMETs on a map of the US: SIERRA (IFR, mountain obscuration), TANGO (turbulence, wind shear, surface wind) and ZULU (icing, freezing level), now and every 3 hours to 12. Tap an area for its altitudes.
  • The path flown now starts at the runway for flights that turn back over their airport after takeoff.

Firmware 4.0.5

  • ALL / AIRLINE / PRIVATE buttons on the left edge of the map filter the traffic, handy when an area is very busy.
  • RADAR / STREET / SAT buttons: the view in use is lit, like FLT / APPR in the flight view.
  • When a plane lands and receivers lose it at the airport, the flight view shows "Landed at LAX - at the gate" instead of "Signal lost".
  • Airport pages show the airport's logo, and PHOTO (now on the INFO tab) shows a real photo of the airport rather than its logo.
  • The path flown no longer includes the aircraft's previous flight.

Firmware 4.0.4

  • Real photos of the actual aircraft (from Planespotters.net, credited to the photographer) instead of a stock photo that could show another airline.
  • When there's no photo of the aircraft, a stock photo in the manufacturer's own colours (Boeing, Airbus, Embraer...), labelled as such.
  • Airline logos next to the airline name in the flight view.
  • FLT (was FIT) shows the whole flight path, including the path actually flown; it's the view a flight opens with.
  • APPR zooms in according to the aircraft's altitude; + and - adjust from there.
  • Flights across the Pacific (for example Taipei - Los Angeles) are now drawn across the date line instead of the long way round the world.

Firmware 4.0.3

  • Fixed the board restarting on wide views (250 and 500 miles) over busy areas: reading thousands of aircraft at once took long enough to trip the watchdog.
  • Fixed memory slowly running out while browsing maps, which caused "Network error" messages and slow or blank maps.
  • Wide views now show aircraft across the whole area. When there are more than 600, an even sample is shown, labelled like "600 of 1,900 aircraft".
  • Aircraft downloads are compressed (about 5 times smaller), so wide views update 2 to 3 times faster, and maps no longer wait for them to finish.

Firmware 4.0.2

  • Fewer "Network error" messages: an aircraft download that breaks off is retried, a single missed update no longer shows an error, and downloads use memory more carefully.
  • Tap the status box (top left) while it shows a problem to retry straight away; it also reconnects Wi-Fi if that has dropped.
  • Faster live map zooming: the map views for the next range in and out are loaded ahead of time, including the steps that skip zoom levels.

Firmware 4.0.1

  • Flight view: origin and destination were sometimes backwards for flights whose flight number is used in both directions, such as out-and-back trips (PHX-LAX-PHX). The aircraft's heading now decides the direction, and a schedule that doesn't match where the plane is going shows "Route not available" instead of a wrong route.
